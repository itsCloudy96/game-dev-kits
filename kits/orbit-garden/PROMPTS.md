# Vibe-coding prompts for Orbit Garden

Use `.cursorrules`, then:

## 1. A little hop
> Add a jump on Space with gravity, so I can hop onto a rock or a low tree stump you place on the island. I should still be stopped at the island rim, and I should not be able to fly.

## 2. A second island
> Add a second, smaller island a short gap away, with two orbs on it. I need a hop or a moving leaf-platform to cross. Keep the camera comfortable on both islands.

## 3. Sundown
> Animate the directional light and sky color so a full collection run goes from late afternoon to dusk. The orbs should stay readable.

## 4. A curious critter
> Add a small original creature (primitives only) that wanders the island and startles away from the craft. No rips from other games. It can be decorative, or it can drop an extra orb the first time I get close.

## 5. First-person toggle
> Press C to swap between the current follow camera and a first-person view from the craft. Remember the last camera in a variable, not localStorage, unless I ask to save it.
