Use `.cursorrules`, then:

## 1. Rebalance mid-game
> The jump from Nebula Loom to Pulsar Farm feels steep. Rebalance GEN so a curious player can reach a Pulsar in a short session without making the first two generators worthless. Show me the old vs new time-to-buy estimates.

## 2. A burst skill
> Add a 'flare' button with a 30-second cooldown that triples click power for 8 seconds. Animate the star during the flare (CSS only, no image files) and show the cooldown.

## 3. Offline crumbs
> When the page loads, grant a little production for the time away, capped at 4 hours, and tell me what I earned. Use timestamps in the existing save object. Still no backend.

## 4. Achievements
> Add 6 local achievements (first generator, first collapse, reach 1k/sec, and so on) with a simple panel. Store unlocks in the save. No pop-up spam — a quiet toast is enough.

## 5. A visual meter
> Draw a canvas or CSS ring around the star that fills with current dust toward the next collapse. Keep the click target easy to hit on a phone.
