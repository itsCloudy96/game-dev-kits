README

Orbit Garden

A tiny 3D collect-a-thon built with Three.js.

Steer a little craft around a floating island, gather the golden orbs, and stay on the grass. Three.js r160 is vendored in vendor/ so the kit runs offline.

Run it

  • Opening index.html as a file often works (the kit uses the UMD build). If the canvas is blank, serve the folder: python3 -m http.server 8080.
  • Controls: WASD or arrows to move. The craft faces the direction of travel. Enter restarts after you collect every orb.

What's inside

  • game.js — scene setup, island, craft, orbs, camera follow. SPEED and ISLAND_R are at the top.
  • vendor/three.min.js — three.js r160, MIT, copyright the three.js authors. See vendor/NOTICE.txt.
  • audio.js, boot.js, style.css.

The island, trees, rocks, and orbs are primitive meshes you can replace or extend.

Remix ideas

PROMPTS.md covers a jump, a day cycle, a second island, and a simple shadow-play camera.

Prompt pack

Copy a prompt into Cursor, Claude, or your coding assistant after you unzip the kit. The same text ships as PROMPTS.md.

Vibe-coding prompts for Orbit Garden

Use .cursorrules, then:

1. A little hop

Add a jump on Space with gravity, so I can hop onto a rock or a low tree stump you place on the island. I should still be stopped at the island rim, and I should not be able to fly.

2. A second island

Add a second, smaller island a short gap away, with two orbs on it. I need a hop or a moving leaf-platform to cross. Keep the camera comfortable on both islands.

3. Sundown

Animate the directional light and sky color so a full collection run goes from late afternoon to dusk. The orbs should stay readable.

4. A curious critter

Add a small original creature (primitives only) that wanders the island and startles away from the craft. No rips from other games. It can be decorative, or it can drop an extra orb the first time I get close.

5. First-person toggle

Press C to swap between the current follow camera and a first-person view from the craft. Remember the last camera in a variable, not localStorage, unless I ask to save it.
