# Vibe-coding prompts for Skyline Dash

Use `.cursorrules`, then:

## 1. Fairer gaps
> Gaps sometimes feel randomly cruel at high speed. Make gap width depend on current speed so a well-timed single jump can still clear them, and keep a few that want a double jump. Add a short comment above the spawn function explaining the rule.

## 2. Coins
> Add collectible coins in the air or on the roof that bump the score and play the coin beep. They should not sit inside a gap. Show coins in the HUD.

## 3. A third stance
> Keep jump and duck, and add a short slide on Down while in the air that lets me slip under a bar I would otherwise hit. Give it a small cooldown.

## 4. Day / night cycle
> Slowly shift the sky palette and window lights over time so a long run feels like dusk, then night, then dawn. No image files.

## 5. Daily seed
> Use the local date (YYYY-MM-DD) to seed the first 20 seconds of obstacles so two people playing the same day see the same opening, then go random. Show the date in the HUD.
