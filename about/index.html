About Cloudy

Cloudy builds free, fast tools for people into AI, AI agents, vibe coding, tech and video games. Game Dev Kits are original starter games you can play, download, and extend with an AI coding assistant.

Also from Cloudy: the AI Tool Finder and the Prompt Library.

How these kits are made

  • Every game is original Cloudy work, MIT-licensed.
  • Art and sound are generated in code. We do not ship copyrighted or trademarked assets.
  • Each zip includes a README, Cursor rules, and a prompt pack meant for vibe-coding the next feature.
  • The 'Pro kits' section is a future idea, not a store. Nothing here costs money today.
